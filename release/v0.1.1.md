its independent-consumer and extension-only reference-fork qualification
directly acquirable from the public release.

## Boundary

Counterparty Evidence emits fixture observations, source descriptors,
timestamps, hashes, availability, ambiguity, and explicit limitations. It does
not issue KYB certificates, establish source truth, make compliance or sanctions
decisions, or use live sources.

The Forge-local WorkProductReceipt remains non-normative. DACS-2 Delivery,
DACS-4 SettlementEvidence, and the DACS-5 graph remain the interoperability
boundary. This release adds no registration, deployment, payment, transfer,
production, certification, steward endorsement, Community listing, or adoption
claim.

The tag is annotated but is not claimed as cryptographically signed. No
maintainer signing identity and consumer trust policy are configured. Immutable
GitHub release attestation and public asset readback remain mandatory.
